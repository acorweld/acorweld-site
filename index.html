About

I'm a welding and coating inspector with over a decade in structural steel — from military engineering vehicles and rail rolling stock to offshore wind foundations. I started in design and process engineering, moved through welding coordination and quality-system building for industrial fabricators, and progressed to Principal Welding Engineer roles managing multiple branches and dozens of welders.

Today I work client-side as a Primary Steel Welding Engineer on one of the North Sea's major offshore wind projects, while building a parallel specialisation in corrosion protection and coating inspection — so I can follow a structure's full quality story, not just half of it.

There's no room for "it'll probably be fine" in industrial quality control. My approach is simple: no compromises. I hold welding and corrosion-protection work to the same standard, so the structures I sign off on are safe, standards-compliant, and built to outlast their design life.

What that combination means for you:

  • One point of contact, not two handovers — the same person who checks your welds also checks the surface prep and coating that protects them.
  • Client-side experience — I know what a project owner needs to see in a report, not just what a fabricator wants to hear.
  • Systems, not just sign-offs — I've built ISO 3834 / EN 1090 certification programmes from scratch, not only audited existing ones, through to successful TÜV audits.

Welding & Coating Inspection Services

Welding Inspection & Supervision

VT/PT/RT witnessing, WPS/WPQR review, welder qualification checks and non-conformity management — embedded in your project team or as periodic client-side oversight.

Coating & Corrosion Protection Inspection

Surface preparation checks, ambient-condition monitoring (temperature, humidity, dew point), DFT measurement, coating-system verification and final acceptance, to ISO 12944, ISO 8501 and NORSOK M-501.

Non-Destructive Testing (NDT)

VT2, PT2 and RT2/RT-F2 to EN ISO 9712, with independent witnessing and reporting.

Client / Third-Party Inspection

Representing your quality interests at a fabricator's or subcontractor's facility — documentation review, in-process inspection and final release decisions.

Quality Systems — Development, Audit & Certification

Building or auditing welding quality systems to ISO 3834, EN 1090 and EN 15085, and preparing fabrication plants for external certification audits.

Technical Training

Welder qualification programmes, WPS/WPQR training, and quality-system onboarding for project teams and production supervisors.
